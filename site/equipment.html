Explore the equipment

  1. On sheet: print the equipment tracking sheet, lay it flat in good light, choose On sheet, and tap Start sheet AR. Allow camera access. Keep the full sheet and border in view.
  2. On floor: choose On floor and tap Start floor AR. Move slowly, aim the gold ring at a clear floor, and tap Place here. This uses estimated floor placement; adjust height if needed.
  3. AR cabin overlays a virtual cabin on your live camera. It stays with the phone and does not use room anchoring. Point your phone around to see your real surroundings.
  4. 3D cabin opens a driving simulation. Drag the view to look around; Center view looks forward. Outside view returns to your previous viewing mode. Switching views reloads the viewer and resets driving.
  5. Enable Drive equipment to drive. Hold Forward or Reverse and a steering button together. Release to stop, or tap Stop. Reset position returns to the starting location. Tracking loss stops driving; press again after tracking returns.
  6. Tap Play to run a cycle. Pause or drag Cycle progress to inspect a movement. Use the joint sliders to move parts with your finger.
  7. Walk slowly around the floor model, or move your phone around the sheet. Move closer to inspect connections. In sheet mode, keep the whole sheet visible; pull back if tracking is lost.
  8. Switching between viewing modes reloads the viewer to restart the camera engine cleanly. In 3D preview, drag to orbit and pinch to zoom.
